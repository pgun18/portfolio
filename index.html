My story

Korean on paper, American by habit, figuring out the rest.

I was born in Korea and moved to the US when I was three. Everything after that, school, friends, my first jobs, happened in Maryland. English is the language I think in.

After high school I went back to Korea for my mandatory military service and finished as a sergeant in a mortar squad. That's where my Korean went from "good enough at home" to actually fluent.

When I got out I didn't really have a plan, so I picked something hard. I flew to Chiang Mai to learn Muay Thai, and instead of staying in an expat bubble I live with a local family. I've had rough stretches (one of my videos is literally called "Why I became homeless at 20"), and a lot of good luck with people who looked out for me.

I don't have my whole career mapped out yet, and I'm okay with that. What I do know: I learn fast, I'm comfortable being the new guy, and I finish what I start.
