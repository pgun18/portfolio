About

One person, several chapters in progress.

I was born in Korea, moved to the United States at age three, and spent my entire childhood and education there. English isn't a second language — it's my language. I graduated from American schools before returning to Korea for mandatory military service.

After finishing service, I found myself in a unique position: fully rooted in Korean society, yet carrying the communication instincts and mindset of someone who grew up American. That duality isn't a complication — it's my edge.

Since then I've kept adding to it: training Muay Thai, building a content channel, and choosing to live with a local family in Chiang Mai, Thailand, rather than staying inside an expat bubble — I'd rather learn a place from the inside than watch it from a distance. That's the pattern I want to keep repeating: picking up beginner Spanish now, and planning to live in several more countries over the coming years, absorbing each culture the way I've absorbed Thai daily life here.

I'm not locking myself into one lane yet — I'm collecting real skills and experience while I work out which combination fits best. I'm young, my resume is still growing, and I know it. But I'm self-aware, hungry, and I show up ready to learn — in whichever language, or wherever, that happens to be.
